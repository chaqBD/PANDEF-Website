About The Summit

Why we're gathering in London.

The UK is home to one of PANDEF Diaspora's most active chapters. The London Summit brings that chapter together with members from across Europe and the wider diaspora — a working session, not just a ceremony — to strengthen the Council's structure abroad and align on priorities for the year ahead.

Full details — date, venue, agenda and how to register — are being finalised by the UK Country Coordinator's office and the Coordinating Council. Rather than publish a placeholder programme, we're keeping this page live and updating it directly as each piece is confirmed.

If you're in the UK or Europe and want to be involved in planning, or simply want to be notified the moment registration opens, join PANDEF Diaspora and note your interest — we'll reach out through the UK chapter.

  • 01

    Chapter Strengthening

    Formalising the UK chapter's structure and local leadership.
  • 02

    Diaspora Coordination

    Aligning members across Europe with the Coordinating Council's priorities.
  • 03

    Community & Culture

    Bringing Niger Delta descendants abroad together in person.
