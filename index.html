Who We Are

An apex voice, extended across the diaspora.

PANDEF — the Pan Niger Delta Forum — is the umbrella socio-political body of the Niger Delta peoples, established in August 2016 to foster inter-ethnic harmony, peace, security and development across the region. PANDEF Diaspora carries that same mandate to Niger Delta communities living abroad.

Through the PANDEF Diaspora Coordinating Council (PDCC), Niger Delta descendants around the world stay connected to home — organising, advocating, and channelling resources and expertise back into the region's development.

We work in step with the National Secretariat and Board of Trustees in Nigeria, serving as a bridge between the homeland and its global community: a second front for the same cause.

PANDEF speaks for the peoples of six Niger Delta states: Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Edo and Rivers.

  • 01

    Peace & Security

    Standing for stability and inter-ethnic harmony across the Niger Delta region.
  • 02

    Equity & Restructuring

    Advocating fair resource control and a more balanced federation.
  • 03

    Regional Development

    Supporting infrastructure, education and economic growth at home.
  • 04

    Diaspora Engagement

    Connecting Niger Delta communities abroad to the movement at home.

How We're Organised

A structure built for accountability.

National Leadership

A chain of accountability, home to abroad.

Authority runs from elder oversight down to the secretariat that manages daily affairs — the same chain PANDEF Diaspora reports into from abroad.

  1. 01

    National Secretariat

    Runs the Forum's day-to-day affairs from the Niger Delta Wetland Centre Complex in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State.
  2. 02

    State Chapters

    Represent each of the six Niger Delta states within the national body.
  3. 03

    Board of Trustees

    Elder statesmen and traditional rulers from across the Niger Delta states, holding final oversight and continuity of the Forum's mandate.
